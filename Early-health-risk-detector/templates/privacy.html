Privacy policy

We keep health information focused on the service you signed up for.

What we collect

Name, email, profile details you provide, and lifestyle logs such as sleep, stress, activity, hydration, screen time, and symptoms.

Why we use it

To generate risk analysis, monthly summaries, trend charts, and product support when you contact us.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal health data and we do not use your entries for unrelated advertising purposes.

Your choices

You can update profile details, stop logging at any time, and contact support to request help with account-related questions.

Retention and access

We retain account and logging data only for product operation, record continuity, and essential support workflows.

Questions

For privacy concerns, write to privacy@healthpulse.com and our team will respond as soon as possible.
