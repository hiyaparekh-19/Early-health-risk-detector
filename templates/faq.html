FAQ

Common questions about logging, analysis, and monthly history.

Straight answers for getting started and understanding what the scores mean.

How many daily logs do I need before predictions appear?

You need at least 7 daily logs in the active month before early prediction appears in Analytics.

Why is the current month not listed in Monthly History?

The active month stays in Analytics so Monthly History can remain focused on completed or older months only.

What do the risk percentages represent?

They represent estimated pattern-based risk levels derived from your logged behaviour, profile inputs, and repeated symptoms.

Can HealthPulse diagnose a medical condition?

No. HealthPulse is a monitoring and insight tool, not a diagnostic service or emergency support channel.

What should I do if a risk score stays high?

Use the score explanation to identify the routine patterns involved, then consult a qualified health professional if symptoms persist or worsen.

How do I contact the team?

You can reach us through the contact page or by emailing care@healthpulse.com.
