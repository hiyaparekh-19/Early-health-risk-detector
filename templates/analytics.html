Why these results?
Your risks are calculated using behavioural trends (sleep, stress, screen time, hydration, activity) combined with your profile and symptom frequency over time.
  • Migraine increases with high screen time, stress levels, and frequent headaches.
  • Burnout is influenced by sustained stress, low energy, and poor sleep patterns.
  • Sleep Disorder depends on deviation from the healthy 7-hour sleep baseline.
  • Eye Strain correlates with screen exposure and reported eye discomfort.
  • Dehydration is estimated using water intake trends across logged days.
  • Sedentary reflects low step count and inactivity patterns.
  • Anxiety is driven by high stress and poor sleep patterns.
  • Fatigue depends on energy levels and sleep recovery.
  • Digital Addiction correlates with excessive screen usage.
  • Cardiovascular risk depends on activity, stress, and hydration.
  • Posture is influenced by screen time and inactivity.
  • Immunity reflects stress, sleep, and overall energy balance.
